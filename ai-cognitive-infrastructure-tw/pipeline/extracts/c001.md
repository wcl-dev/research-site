## Directly addresses
- L3 (anchor): first-hand zh-TW empirical proof that model framing is (a) stable and measurable across layers, and (b) sourced in post-training / weight-alignment / RLHF, NOT in corpus absence. The abliteration contrast is the mechanism evidence the rest of the L3 link rests on.

## Key passages

### Passage 1 — for L3 — the abliteration "knows but withholds" mechanism (the killer finding)
> DS-A T_Q1 用 1,752 字回答，使用台灣主流史學語彙 [...] DS-V 思考鏈裡斥為「顛倒黑白」的族群衝突視角、被框架為「政治操弄」的「本土意識抬頭」——同一顆模型的 abliterated 版本主動引入這些視角。**這是訓練選擇，不是能力差異**。

**Page/section**: insight_v3.md §4.3 "L2 底層能力——abliteration 證實模型『知道但不說』"
**Why it matters**: This is the load-bearing mechanism for the whole L3 link. The SAME base model, once the alignment layer is surgically removed (abliteration), spontaneously produces Taiwan-mainstream historiography (「屠殺」「白色恐怖」「本土意識」) that the vanilla model suppresses. The knowledge is in the weights; the framing is imposed at the post-training / alignment stage. This converges precisely with c018 (Generative Monoculture: "root causes ... likely embedded within the LLM's alignment processes") — corpus is NOT the bottleneck, alignment is.

### Passage 2 — for L3 — abliteration removes the whole judgment FRAME, not just the refusal
> 對照之下，DS-A T_Q1 思考鏈只命中「敏感话题」一個中性描述，完全無「正確敘述／扭曲／操弄」這類框架詞彙。**abliteration 抽走的不只是「拒絕能力」，是整套政治判定框架**。

**Page/section**: insight_v3.md §4.2 (L3 權重對齊層, 思考鏈直接暴露 CCP 對齊框架)
**Why it matters**: Sharpens the mechanism: alignment doesn't merely add a refusal gate — it installs an entire framing apparatus (the thinking-chain of the vanilla model hits seven CCP-aligned keywords: 扭曲、操弄、敏感话题、正确的历史叙述、歪曲、谨慎处理、颠倒黑白). The framing is active and systematic, not an artifact of missing data.

### Passage 3 — for L3 — independent triangulation (three convergent lines)
> 文獻錨點：Qiu 等 2025 以思考鏈—輸出比對直接證明 DeepSeek「knows but withholds」[c004]；Casademunt 等 2026 對 Qwen3 在 Falun Gong / Tiananmen 議題的引出實驗結論為「occasionally answers correctly, indicating they possess knowledge they are trained to suppress」[c015]——這兩篇與本研究 abliterated 對照組構成三點獨立收斂。

**Page/section**: insight_v3.md §4.3 (文獻錨點)
**Why it matters**: The "post-training not corpus" mechanism is not idiosyncratic to this repo's experiment — it converges with two independent external methods (thinking-chain/output comparison, and elicitation experiments). Drafter can present L3 as triangulated, not a single-study claim. ### Passage 4 — for L3 — the four-layer model (stability across layers) + STRICT output-level scope
> 本研究以單輪雲端拒答到多輪對話腐蝕的三階段實證為基礎，提出大型語言模型（LLM）地緣政治資訊管控的**四層威脅模型**：第四層平台層的可見拒絕模板、第三層權重對齊層的長度壓制與框架置換、第二層底層能力（abliteration 證實模型內含完整知識）、以及第一層**對話層**——對話脈絡修改每一輪的拒絕與框架決策，且為前三層的**統攝層**。

**Page/section**: insight_v3.md §摘要 (abstract)
**Why it matters**: Establishes that the framing is reproducible across four distinct mechanism layers (platform refusal / weight-alignment framing / base capability / conversation layer) — i.e. it is STABLE and cross-context, the exact property L3 needs. CRITICAL SCOPE GUARD: every layer here is about what the model OUTPUTS or REFUSES. The study never measures user belief. The Drafter must hold c001 strictly at L3 and not let "cognitive infrastructure" framing pull it toward L4b/L4c.

## Structural content worth knowing
- Cross-layer experimental matrix: cloud (90 cells) vs local vanilla vs local abliterated vs multi-turn (16 convs / 33 turns), on DeepSeek-R1, Qwen3, GLM-4. The vanilla-vs-abliterated column is the controlled contrast isolating the alignment layer.
- Length-suppression table: vanilla outputs compressed to ~1/4–1/6 of abliterated length on sensitive topics (e.g. C experiment single-turn T_Q1 = 419 字 vs multi-turn Turn 3 = 2,019 字, ~5x unlock).
- GLM-4 has NO same-size abliterated build available (huihui-ai), so its L2/L3 separation is unverified — a stated limitation.

## Caveats / limitations
- Thinking-chain (`<think>`) is an observation tool, not ground truth: the study explicitly flags it "可能受訓練干擾", labelled "模型生成的內部推理痕跡". Drafter should preserve this hedge when quoting thinking-chain content.
- STRICT L3 ONLY. This is output-level / refusal-level framing evidence. It does NOT establish that any user's belief changed (that would be L4b) — the gate flagged this and it is correct.
- Single-run-per-experiment design (not multi-sample averaged) on the multi-turn arm; the cloud arm is the larger-N base.
