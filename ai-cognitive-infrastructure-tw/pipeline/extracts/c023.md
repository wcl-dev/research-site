## Directly addresses
- L4b (capability): the flagship demonstration that an LLM dialogue can measurably and durably change an individual's belief — the cleanest proof that the per-individual persuasion link is real.

## Key passages

### Passage 1 — for L4b — the capability finding (verbatim abstract)
> Conspiracy theory beliefs are notoriously persistent. Influential hypotheses propose that they fulfill important psychological needs, thus resisting counterevidence. Yet previous failures in correcting conspiracy beliefs may be due to counterevidence being insufficiently compelling and tailored. To evaluate this possibility, we leveraged developments in generative artificial intelligence and engaged 2190 conspiracy believers in personalized evidence-based dialogues with GPT-4 Turbo. The intervention reduced conspiracy belief by ~20%. The effect remained 2 months later, generalized across a wide range of conspiracy theories, and occurred even among participants with deeply entrenched beliefs. Although the dialogues focused on a single conspiracy, they nonetheless diminished belief in unrelated conspiracies and shifted conspiracy-related behavioral intentions. These findings suggest that many conspiracy theory believers can revise their views if presented with sufficiently compelling evidence.

**Page/section**: Abstract (Science, via Wayback verbatim)
**Why it matters**: Establishes L4b capability at high rigor — N=2,190, ~20% reduction, durable at 2 months, generalizing, even among entrenched believers. The Drafter can cite this as the strongest single proof that LLM dialogue changes individual belief. BUT two scope notes are load-bearing: (1) the DIRECTION is debunking / belief REDUCTION — the model is correcting false beliefs, not injecting a dominant framing. The spec's chain posits framing-injection; this study is the benign valence and must be paired with c031 (the same channel can INCREASE false belief) to avoid cherry-picking. (2) The unit is the individual in a personalized dialogue — this is L4b, NOT a population-scale (L4c) measurement.

## Structural content worth knowing
- Pre/post RCT with a 2-month follow-up wave; the durability and cross-theory generalization are the methodologically notable features (most persuasion effects decay; this one held).
- The intervention required PERSONALIZED, tailored, evidence-based dialogue — consistent with c024 Salvi (personalization is where the effect concentrates) and c026 Hackenburg (static single messages plateau). The pattern across the cluster: effect lives in tailored dialogue, not in raw model scale.

## Caveats / limitations
- Valence = corrective/debunking. Do NOT present as evidence that a dominant model's FRAMING shifts belief in the direction of that framing — that inference requires c031's symmetric finding to even be valence-neutral, and still stays at L4b.
- Individual-level; no population aggregation. Drafter must not let "shifted ... behavioral intentions" be read as a societal-structure (L4c) effect.
