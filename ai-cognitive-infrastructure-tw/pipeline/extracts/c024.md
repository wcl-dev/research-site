## Directly addresses

## Key passages

### Passage 1 — for L4b — the conditional effect (verbatim abstract)
> In this pre-registered study, we analyze the effect of AI-driven persuasion in a controlled, harmless setting. We create a web-based platform where participants engage in short, multiple-round debates with a live opponent. Each participant is randomly assigned to one of four treatment conditions, corresponding to a two-by-two factorial design: (1) Games are either played between two humans or between a human and an LLM; (2) Personalization might or might not be enabled, granting one of the two players access to basic sociodemographic information about their opponent. We found that participants who debated GPT-4 with access to their personal information had 81.7% (p < 0.01; N=820 unique participants) higher odds of increased agreement with their opponents compared to participants who debated humans. Without personalization, GPT-4 still outperforms humans, but the effect is lower and statistically non-significant (p=0.31). Overall, our results suggest that concerns around personalization are meaningful and have important implications for the governance of social media and the design of new online environments.

**Page/section**: Abstract (arXiv:2403.14380, verbatim)
**Why it matters**: The headline ("GPT-4 more persuasive than humans") is TRUE only with personalization: 81.7% higher odds WITH sociodemographic access, but "the effect is lower and statistically non-significant (p=0.31)" WITHOUT it. The Drafter must quote the conditionality — the persuasion edge is a personalization effect, not a generic model-framing effect. This matters for the chain: the spec's L4b posits homogenized output shifting belief; this study says the shift depends on per-target tailoring, which is the OPPOSITE of homogenized one-to-many output. It bounds L4b toward the personalized-microtargeting case, not the mass-broadcast case.

## Structural content worth knowing
- Clean 2×2 factorial (human vs LLM) × (personalization on vs off), N=820 — the design directly isolates the personalization moderator, which is its distinctive contribution versus c023/c030.
- The authors' own framing of implications is "governance of social media and the design of new online environments" — i.e. microtargeting infrastructure, NOT population-scale cognition. Keep this scope.

## Caveats / limitations
- Effect is individual debate-level (L4b), conditional on personalization. Do not generalize to homogenized mass output (which is the L4a→L4b path the chain actually needs).
- access_status: nature.com bounced through an IDP cookie-auth loop; abstract is verbatim from the authors' open arXiv version of the same pre-registered RCT. The figures (81.7%, p<0.01, N=820, p=0.31) are from that abstract.
- Minor divergence note: the candidate said "GPT-4 with access to personal info is more persuasive than humans" — TRUE, but the candidate omitted that the NON-personalized effect is statistically non-significant. The Drafter should add that nuance; the headline alone over-reads.
