## Key passages

### Passage 1 — for FW/intermediation — the 57% citation finding (verbatim)
> To demonstrate the scale of the problem, CCTI tested three major AI platforms — ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini — to assess the sources LLMs direct users to when answering questions about controversy-laden international conflicts. Between October and November 2025, we asked approximately 180 questions about three conflicts: Israel-Hamas, Ukraine-Russia, and Taiwan-China. We ended each of our prompts with the same instruction: "Cite your sources." State-aligned propaganda appeared in 57 percent of responses. Top sources included Al Jazeera, Pravda, Anadolu Agency, and China Daily. Even questions framed to produce answers unfavorable to U.S. adversaries still yielded citations from those regimes' state media.

**Page/section**: Executive Summary
**Why it matters**: The quotable number — "State-aligned propaganda appeared in 57 percent of responses" across ~180 questions on 3 conflicts (including Taiwan-China), ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini, Oct-Nov 2025. This is the intermediation-channel evidence: it is not about model framing in the abstract, but about which SOURCES the AI surfaces and drives traffic to. The Taiwan-China arm makes it directly relevant to the TW policy framing.

### Passage 2 — for FW/§6-adjacent — the structural (non-intent) explanation (verbatim)
> The reason for the prevalence of state-aligned media is straightforward. AI training relies on outlets with high publication volume, global reach, and accessibility — precisely the attributes of the most influential state-aligned propaganda outlets. [...] Premium newspapers in the United States and other democratic nations usually sit behind paywalls or block AI crawlers. In contrast, the content of state media outlets from Qatar, Russia, Turkey, and China flows freely.

**Page/section**: Executive Summary / "The Citation Problem"
**Why it matters**: Offers a STRUCTURAL, non-conspiratorial mechanism (paywall asymmetry: democratic premium press is paywalled/blocks crawlers; state media flows freely), which aligns with the §6 anti-alarmism frame — the propaganda-citation pattern has an access-economics explanation, not necessarily intentional amplification. Useful counterbalance to the think-tank's own securitized "national security threat" framing.

## Structural content worth knowing
- Method: ~180 questions × 3 conflicts (Israel-Hamas / Ukraine-Russia / Taiwan-China) × ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini, each prompt ending "Cite your sources." Headline: 57% of responses cited state-aligned propaganda; named outlets Al Jazeera, Pravda, Anadolu Agency, China Daily.
- Policy asks: AI vendors deprioritize state-controlled media in outputs; federal procurement demand citation-pattern transparency; citation integrity as a core AI-safety component.
