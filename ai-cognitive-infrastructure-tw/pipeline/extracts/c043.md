## Key passages

### Passage 1 — for §6 — framing is mutable + data-attributable, and cross-origin convergence (verbatim abstract)
> [...] We probe 10 LLMs with 63 items from the Hofstede Values Survey Module and World Values Survey, translated into 11 languages, and formulated as prompts with and without different explicit cultural perspectives. Our study confirms that both prompt language and cultural perspective produce variation in LLM outputs, but with an important caveat: While targeted prompting can, to a certain extent, steer LLM responses in the direction of the predominant values of the corresponding countries, it does not overcome the models' systematic bias toward the values associated with a restricted set of countries in our dataset: the Netherlands, Germany, the US, and Japan. All tested models, regardless of their origin, exhibit remarkably similar patterns: They produce fairly neutral responses on most topics, with selective progressive stances on issues such as social tolerance. [...] These findings reveal that LLMs occupy an uncomfortable middle ground: They are responsive enough to changes in prompts to produce variation, but too firmly anchored to specific cultural defaults to adequately represent cultural diversity.

**Page/section**: Abstract (arXiv:2511.03980, verbatim; bracketed ellipses omit the intro and the alignment-method comparison sentences)
**Why it matters**: (a) §6 use: "both prompt language and cultural perspective produce variation in LLM outputs" — framing is mutable/context-elicited, so a fixed-political-intent reading is too strong; and the residual bias is "toward the values associated with a restricted set of countries ... the Netherlands, Germany, the US, and Japan" — i.e. attributable to training-data imbalance (Western/developed-nation overrepresentation), not a China-specific design conspiracy. (b) L4a use: "All tested models, regardless of their origin, exhibit remarkably similar patterns" — a direct cross-origin CONVERGENCE finding (homogenization toward shared defaults), independent of c018. The Drafter can cite this both to defuse the directional-artifact concern AND as a homogenization datapoint.

## Structural content worth knowing
- 10 LLMs × 63 Hofstede/WVS items × 11 languages × (with/without explicit cultural framing). The "anchored to NL/DE/US/JP defaults despite prompt steering" is the load-bearing result — bias is steerable at the margin but not eliminable, and it points at data composition.

## Caveats / limitations
- Preprint (qs=3). The cross-origin-convergence claim is one study; pair with c018 for the homogenization point and with c042 for the alignment-artifact point.
- Measures values-survey responses (output), not user belief. The §6 use (mutability, data-attribution) is its primary load; the L4a convergence use is secondary.
