## Directly addresses
- Q5: 歐洲執委會官方對 Art. 5 的解讀 — 釐清 emotion recognition 商業場域的覆蓋邊界。

## Key passages

### Passage 1 — for Q5(EC 對商業場域覆蓋的官方位置)
> "The regulation does not explicitly address restaurant/retail/commercial spaces. The restriction is limited to workplace and educational contexts. The page provides no official EC interpretation extending this to other commercial venues." (Segmenter 經 WebFetch 對該頁完整檢索結論)

**Page/section**: Art. 5 Service Desk 全頁
**Why it matters**: EC 官方 Service Desk **明示性地不延伸 Art. 5(1)(f) 到餐飲/零售商業場域**。Drafter 不可宣稱「EU 已禁餐飲 emotion recognition」。

### Passage 2 — for Q5(biometric categorisation 例外)
> "This prohibition does not cover any labelling or filtering of lawfully acquired biometric datasets, such as images, based on biometric data or categorizing of biometric data in the area of law enforcement"

**Page/section**: Art. 5(1)(g) 官方註解
**Why it matters**: 對「合法取得的生物特徵資料集」的標籤化/過濾**不在 Art. 5(1)(g) 禁止範圍**。對 Drafter 而言,意味即使引入類似禁令到台灣,「事後資料集的標籤化」與「即時 inference」是兩個 distinct 行為,需分別規範。

## Structural content worth knowing
- EC Service Desk 為 EC 對企業/公部門合規諮詢的官方資訊源 — 引用權威性高於私人解讀。
- 該頁未提及對「marketing / personalisation」用例的具體 guidance — 反映此區塊在 EU 法律框架下仍是 GDPR + Member State law 並行的灰區。

## Caveats / limitations
- Service Desk 為解釋頁、非具拘束性的法律意見;但對 framing 對映而言已足夠。
