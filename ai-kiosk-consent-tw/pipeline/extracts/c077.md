# c077: EDPB Opinion 11/2024 on the use of facial recognition to streamline airport passengers' flow

**URL**: https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/2024-05/edpb_opinion_202411_facialrecognitionairports_en.pdf
**Source type**: 監管機構正式 Opinion (EDPB) | **Quality**: qs=5
**Deep-read on**: 2026-05-27 (Layer B `curl + pdftotext`)
**Access status**: ok
**Pages/length**: 1475-行 pdftotext extract (前 30 頁)

## Directly addresses
- Q5 (國際最低標準對照): GDPR Art. 9 對 biometric data for identification 的 explicit consent 要求 — 與 BIPA (c073) 形成「EU + US-IL」雙標準對照。
- Q4 (法律屬性對照): EDPB 確認 biometric data 屬 special category 受 Art. 9 保護 — 對台灣個資法 §6 (人臉聲紋未列特種個資) 的核心對照論點。
- Q5 (consent 機制): 「Individuals would need to be able to easily withdraw such consent at any time」 — 撤回層 (D 軸第三層) 的 EU 標準要求,台灣 A 區 zero。

## Key passages

### Passage 1 — for Q5 (Art. 9 special category)
> 「The processing of biometric data which is granted special protection under Article 9 GDPR. Before using such technologies, even if they were to be considered particularly effective, controllers should assess the impact on data subjects' fundamental rights and freedoms and consider whether less intrusive means may achieve their legitimate purpose of the processing.」

**Page/section**: EDPB Opinion para 4 (pdftotext verbatim, lines 28-32)
**Why it matters**: **GDPR Art. 9 把 biometric data 列為 special category 是 EU 與台灣個資法 §6 的核心對照點**。台灣個資法第 6 條把「病歷、醫療、基因、性生活、健康檢查、犯罪前科」列為特種個資;**人臉、聲紋、指紋未列入** — 與 GDPR Art. 9 範圍 mismatch。Drafter 在 Q4 法律拆解 / Q5 國際對照可指出:**台灣個資法第 6 條範圍與 GDPR Art. 9 的歷史落差,正是 A∩D 灰區存在的條文層原因**;PDPC 籌備處引虹膜為例的論證邏輯 (參 brief F4 seed) 是「即使未列特種個資,因高識別力仍受一般個資保護」— 對應 GDPR 框架,此論證邏輯接近 EDPB「even if considered effective, assess less intrusive means」的比例原則 + impact assessment 要求。

### Passage 2 — for Q5 (explicit consent + withdraw)
> 「If the relevant controllers were to rely on this legal basis, they would need to obtain a valid explicit consent from the individuals willing to use such services. Such explicit consent would need to be freely given, specific and informed and whether those conditions are met would be analysed on a case by case basis. This, inter alia, means that:
> 1) Individuals would need to be able to easily withdraw such consent at any time and ...
> 3) Explicit consent would also need to be sought from individuals whose biometric data are processed, even if they have not enrolled to be identified or authenticated by such means. In other words, it is essential that individuals who did not explicitly consent to facial recognition for the purpose intended would not have their faces scanned by」

**Page/section**: EDPB Opinion paras 15-17 (pdftotext verbatim, lines 416-456)
**Why it matters**: **EDPB 對「explicit consent」標準的具體拆解**:(a) **freely given, specific, informed** — 三條件並列,對應台灣個資法 §7 同意要件,但台灣條文未明確「explicit」(允 implied consent);(b) **easily withdraw at any time** — D 軸第三層 (撤回) 的明確要求,**對應台灣 A 區 zero (Q3 結構性零)**;(c) **「沒同意者的臉也不應被掃描」** — 對應台灣 kiosk「站到 kiosk 前就被掃描」此設計性「無 opt-out 預設」根本違反 EU 標準。Drafter 在 Q5 對照表三層 (告知/同意/撤回) 對台灣 vs EU 評分時,EU 在三層皆 explicit 高標準、台灣 A 區三層皆 zero — 落差全面。

### Passage 3 — for Q4 (biometric template sole control 原則)
> 「The intrusiveness of the processing can be counterbalanced by the active involvement of the passengers as their biometric template is stored in their hands only, for example, on their individual device, under their sole control and their data is deleted shortly after the matching is completed.」

**Page/section**: EDPB Opinion para 14 (pdftotext verbatim, lines 56-60)
**Why it matters**: **EDPB「biometric template 在 data subject sole control」原則** — 是 EU 對 biometric data 處理的核心 design principle:儲存控制在當事人 (例如:存在 individual device 上)、處理完即刪除。對應台灣 A 區 kiosk: **vendor 完全掌握 inference 流程,當事人 zero control over biometric processing**。Drafter 在 Q5 對照 + Q7 政策建議可指出:**EU sole control 原則 implementable 路徑(template on personal device + 即時刪除) 在當前 kiosk 設計下完全未實作** — 台灣 vendor 設計 default 即與 EU 立場相反。此「設計層 ↔ 法律層落差」是 Drafter 在政策建議的核心結構性論點。

## Structural content worth knowing
- EDPB Opinion 結構:Background → Question 1 (device-stored template scenario, Scenario 1) → Question 2 (centralised template + key in user hand, Scenario 2 / Scenario 3-4 部分否定)。Drafter 若需完整四場景對照可全篇引;Q5 對照只需引用核心原則部分。
- EDPB 對 biometric identification vs biometric categorisation 的區分在 Opinion 文本中比 EU AI Act Art. 5 較含蓄,主要 paragraph 21-22 略述兩 functions「distinct but both rely on biometric data」皆屬 Art. 9 special category — 對 Drafter Q4 / Q5 拆解「inference 是不是 biometric data」直接有用。
- 「Article 9(4) GDPR provides that Member States may maintain or introduce further conditions, including limitations, with regard to the processing of biometric data」— 各成員國可加嚴。對 Drafter Q5 / 政策建議:**EU 留 Member State 加嚴空間 = 即使 Art. 9 通則,各國仍可加嚴 (如義大利 GPDP 對 retail FR 多次罰款)**;對應台灣 PDPC 籌備處未來可借鏡此「中央通則 + 加嚴 sectoral 指引」結構。
- Recital 51/52/53 GDPR + Art. 4(11)/(14)、Art. 7、Art. 9(2)(a) 是 cross-reference 條文清單 — Drafter 若需深入 conditional consent / withdraw 條文細節可追到 GDPR 原文。

## Caveats / limitations
- EDPB Opinion 主題為機場 FR (passenger flow streamlining),非餐飲 inference;Drafter 引用時要區分「機場 scenario 結論」(可能更嚴,因 boarding 流程的強制性) vs「跨場景核心原則」(template sole control / explicit consent / withdraw / 沒同意者不掃描)。
- EDPB Opinion 對 demographic categorisation 處理較簡略;若 Drafter 需 categorisation-specific 立場可同時引 EDPB 對 retail FR 的其他 statements / 對 EU AI Act Art. 5 emotion recognition + biometric categorisation 禁令 (c067/c068/c069) 的對應。
- pdftotext extract 為前 30 頁,Opinion 完整版可能 60+ 頁;若 Drafter 需 Scenarios 3-4 細節可請 operator 追加。
- 「biometric template sole control」原則是 EDPB 對「reduced intrusiveness」的 design pattern recommendation,非 mandatory standard;controller 仍可選擇 centralised storage,但要證明 less intrusive means 不可行。
