# c008: The rise of the disinformation-for-hire industry (EUvsDisinfo / EEAS)

**URL**: https://euvsdisinfo.eu/the-rise-of-the-disinformation-for-hire-industry/
**Source type**: gov (EEAS / EUvsDisinfo) | **Quality**: qs=4
**Deep-read on**: 2026-06-16
**Access status**: ok (curl browser-UA; WebFetch 403'd, no Wayback snapshot)
**Pages/length**: ~12.4K chars article body

## Directly addresses
- Q1 [動機合流]: 國家經營的 IO 已外包給販售「造謠即服務」的私人公司,客戶橫跨政黨/企業/國家——P/S 行為者邊界模糊的機構級論述。
- Q2 [共用 TTP]: Team Jorge / AIMS 軟體能批量生成數千假帳號(含合成照片、傳記、背景故事)——bulk persona 生產的具名實例。
- Q7 (邊緣): 點名 information laundering 的洗白機制(passing through private firms, fake accounts, proxy media)。

## Key passages

### Passage 1 — for Q1 (mercenary / swappable client)
> "A quiet revolution has taken place in the world of propaganda. Operations that used to be run by authoritarian governments and intelligence agencies are now outsourced to private firms that sell disinformation and deception as a service."

**Page/section**: 開頭段
**Why it matters**: IO 從國家功能轉為可購買服務的核心命題句——直接支撐「動機是基礎設施上的可替換 payload」。

### Passage 2 — for Q1 (client mix = political + corporate)
> "The firm claimed to have interfered in 33 presidential elections, winning 27 of them. Its clients included political parties, corporations, and, allegedly, state-linked actors."

**Page/section**: "Team Jorge and the commercialisation of deception"
**Why it matters**: 同一供應商(Team Jorge)的客戶同時涵蓋政黨、企業與國家連結行為者——P 與 S 在供給側合流的最具體單一句。

### Passage 3 — for Q2 (bulk persona infrastructure)
> "At the heart of Team Jorge's system was Advanced Impact Media Solutions (AIMS), software capable of creating and coordinating thousands of fake social-media accounts, complete with synthetic photos, biographies, and backstories. These avatars could be mobilised to flood debates, spread narratives, or harass opponents."

**Page/section**: "Team Jorge..."
**Why it matters**: 批量身分生產(synthetic photos/bios)＋ flooding 用途——I 軸基礎設施與 CD「以量淹沒辯論」的橋接證據。

### Passage 4 — for Q1/Q7 (information laundering)
> "— hiding the true origin of disinformation by passing it through private firms, fake accounts, and proxy media. As these actors repeat and amplify the message, it begins to look organic and locally produced."

**Page/section**: 段落(information laundering)
**Why it matters**: cloaking/洗白機制掏空「來源看起來正常」的判斷依據——直接對位 CD 的 examples_in。

### Passage 5 — for Q3 (AI as force multiplier — but in EEAS's narrower sense)
> "AI allows campaigns that once required hundreds of people to be run by a handful of operators or even a single individual. What once took a troll farm and a whole building in St. Petersburg now takes a laptop."

**Page/section**: "Automation and AI: the new force multiplier"
**Why it matters**: 邊際成本崩塌的鮮明措辭(Q3)。**Caveat**: EEAS 在此用 "force multiplier" 指 AI 對人力的放大,**不是**本研究 FM(認知破壞→後續操作成功率)的意義——Drafter 引用須標明語義差。

## Caveats / limitations
- EEAS/EUvsDisinfo 是歐盟對外機構的對外傳播產品,frame 強烈偏 FIMI(外國性/威權國家),對「純獲利、零外國性」的協同操作著墨少——它證的是 P 客戶與商業供應商合流,**不**直接證 S(純詐騙)端。
- 屬 policy-position,非實證研究;Team Jorge 數字("interfered in 33 elections")為該公司自我宣稱,文中已用 "claimed"。
- "force multiplier" 一詞語義與本研究 FM 不同(見 Passage 5 caveat),避免術語混淆。
