# c019: The Russian "Firehose of Falsehood" Propaganda Model (RAND PE-198, Paul & Matthews, 2016)

**URL**: https://www.rand.org/pubs/perspectives/PE198.html (PDF: RAND_PE198.pdf)
**Source type**: think_tank (RAND) | **Quality**: qs=5
**Deep-read on**: 2026-06-16
**Access status**: ok (Layer B — direct PDF curl; WebFetch 403'd, Wayback HTML also available 2026-06-06)
**Pages/length**: ~16pp

## Directly addresses
- Q4 [認知破壞 vs 既有框架]: firehose 是「以海量、多管道內容淹沒」最權威的既有理論——CD 必須對位它並指出補充了什麼。
- Q5 [FM 證據狀態]: firehose 的有效性建立在個人說服心理學(多來源/重複→可信),這是 FM 鏈的一個已有實證環,但停在個人信念層、未及母體信任基線。

## Key passages

### Passage 1 — for Q4 (四特徵,CD 須對位的框架定義)
> The model's four distinctive features: "1. High-Volume and Multichannel ... 2. Rapid, Continuous, and Repetitive ... 3. Lacks commitment to objective reality ... 4. Lacks commitment to consistency."

**Page/section**: 模型四特徵小節標題(逐字)
**Why it matters**: CD「無差別飽和資訊環境」最接近的既有框架。Drafter 對位時:firehose 仍是**為了推某套敘事**(payload 有方向);CD 主張傷害本身**獨立於任何特定敘事**(飽和即傷害)——這是補充點。

### Passage 2 — for Q4/Q5 (量何以有效 — 個人說服心理學)
> "Multiple sources are more persuasive than a single source, especially if those sources contain different arguments that point to the same conclusion." 以及 "Receiving the same or similar message from multiple sources is more persuasive." 以及 "Repeated exposure to a statement has been shown to increase its acceptance as true."

**Page/section**: "High-Volume and Multichannel" 旁的 persuasion-research 條列
**Why it matters**: 這是 FM 鏈「量→效果」**已有實證**的一環——但證據止於**個人對單一敘事的接受度**(belief acceptance),不涵蓋「母體信任基線下降使後續操作更易」。Drafter 可用此標記 FM 鏈哪環有證、哪環是 gap。

### Passage 3 — for Q4 (重複→熟悉→接受;censorship-by-noise 的近親)
> "repetition leads to familiarity, and familiarity leads to acceptance"

**Page/section**: "Rapid, Continuous, and Repetitive"
**Why it matters**: 重複的認知機制句,可與 c021(censorship through noise / flooding)並列為 CD「壓低碰到可信來源機率」的雙理論源。

### Passage 4 — for Q4/Q6 (傳統反制無效 — 支撐 XD 必要性)
> "several of these features run directly counter to the conventional wisdom on effective influence and communication from government or defense sources, which traditionally emphasize the importance of truth, credibility, and the avoidance" (of inconsistency).

**Page/section**: 模型特徵總結段
**Why it matters**: 傳統(單域、以真相為本)反制法對 firehose 無效——間接支撐 brief「動機分工/單一方法防治有結構盲區、需 XD」。

## Structural content worth knowing
- 四特徵 × 對應 persuasion-research 條列的對照表結構(每個特徵旁邊列出支撐的心理學發現)——Drafter 可引此表結構說明 firehose「以心理學為地基」。
- 末節 "Don't expect to counter the firehose of falsehood with the squirt gun of truth" 的著名比喻(反制建議段),點明 fact-check 跟不上量。

## Caveats / limitations
- 2016 文獻,前生成式 AI;描述俄式人力 troll factory 模型,未含 AI slop 規模躍升(Q3 須由 c024/c079 補)。
- 框架的效果端是**個人信念接受**,非母體層 epistemic commons——這正是 CD 主張要補充的位置,Drafter 須明說 CD≠firehose。
