# c020: Deep Fakes — A Looming Challenge for Privacy, Democracy, and National Security (Chesney & Citron, 2019)

**URL**: https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3213954 (SSRN);引用文字取自開放版 California Law Review (107 Calif. L. Rev. 1753)
**Source type**: peer_reviewed (法學評論) | **Quality**: qs=5
**Deep-read on**: 2026-06-16
**Access status**: ok (Layer A on CLR OA HTML mirror; SSRN abstract 403'd + no Wayback, CLR published version is identical text)
**Pages/length**: long law-review article(僅抽 liar's dividend 段)

## Directly addresses
- Q4 [認知破壞 vs 既有框架]: liar's dividend 是「合成媒體普及→真實證據可被指為假」的經典框架,CD 須對位它。
- Q5 [FM 機制與證據狀態]: liar's dividend 給出一條「資訊環境劣化→信任基線下降→特定行為者得利(逃避問責)」的**理論**鏈,是 FM 的一個近親機制——但屬推論,非實證。

## Key passages

### Passage 1 — for Q4 (liar's dividend 核心定義,逐字)
> "Ironically, liars aiming to dodge responsibility for their real words and actions will become more credible as the public becomes more educated about the threats posed by deep fakes. ... As the public becomes more aware of the idea that video and audio can be convincingly faked, some will try to escape accountability for their actions by denouncing authentic video and audio as deep fakes. Put simply: a skeptical public will be primed to doubt the authenticity of real audio and video evidence. ... Hence what we call the liar's dividend: this dividend flows, perversely, in proportion to success in educating the public about the dangers of deep fakes."

**Page/section**: "The Liar's Dividend: Beware the Cry of Deep-Fake News"
**Why it matters**: 紅利「與公眾警覺程度成正比」這個悖論,是 CD 對位的關鍵——它把傷害定位在**整個公眾的辨識基線**(skeptical public primed to doubt),而非被騙個人。這幾乎就是 CD「壓低母體辨識基線」的法學前身;Drafter 應講清 CD 把這個個案機制**一般化**為基礎設施層的無差別傷害。

### Passage 2 — for Q4/CD (真假無差別掏空判斷)
> "This skepticism can be invoked just as well against authentic as against adulterated content."

**Page/section**: 同段
**Why it matters**: 一句點出「懷疑對真內容與假內容同樣有效」——正是 CD「真假混雜 + cloaking 掏空『來源看起來正常』判斷依據」的機制核句。

### Passage 3 — for Q5/FM (信任基線是更大趨勢的一部分 — 但屬推論)
> "The liar's dividend would run with the grain of larger trends involving truth skepticism. Most notably, recent years have seen mounting distrust of traditional sources of news."

**Page/section**: 同段續
**Why it matters**: 作者把 liar's dividend 接到「對傳統新聞來源信任下降」的宏觀趨勢——這是 FM 鏈「信任基線下降」環的理論連結,但 Chesney/Citron **未提供量測**,Drafter 須標為推論環(誠實標記 Q5 邊界)。

## Structural content worth knowing
- 文章先列舉 deepfake 的各類傷害(個人/社會/國安),再以 liar's dividend 作為「不同種類」的傷害收尾——這個「affirmative lies vs denial lies」二分結構,可作 Drafter 區分 CD 兩種作用(注入 vs 掏空判斷)的引用支架。

## Caveats / limitations
- 法學評論,屬理論/質性論證,非實證量測;liar's dividend 的規模未被量化。
- 2019 文獻、以 deepfake(視訊/音訊偽造)為背景,前 LLM-slop 時代;CD 要把這個「單一行為者得利」機制推廣到「無特定行為者的基礎設施層飽和」——這是 Drafter 須明說的補充與差異。
