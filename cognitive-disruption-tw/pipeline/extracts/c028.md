## Key passages

### Passage 1 — for Q7 (IMS 定義 + 開發者)
> IMS = "a set of adversarial behaviours, tools, and tactics likely linked to the same threat actor" — "The IMS model, developed by France's VIGINUM and agreed upon with the European External Action Service".

**Page/section**: 定義段
**Why it matters**: XD 共用交換接口的具名來源與權威背書(VIGINUM + EEAS 共識)。

### Passage 2 — for Q7 (三層分析,逐字)
> "These sets bridge the gap between individual incidents and full campaign attribution, enabling analysis at three levels: tactical (incident-level data), operational (narratives and infrastructure), and strategic (linking to threat actors and intent)."

**Page/section**: 框架說明
**Why it matters**: 三層(tactical/operational/strategic)的逐字定義。**對位本研究**:operational 層 = "narratives and infrastructure" 把基礎設施與敘事並列為操作層分析單位——這是 IMS 與本研究 I 軸最接近的對接點。Drafter 可主張本研究的 CD/I 命題正落在 IMS 的 operational 層、但補上「動機可替換」的解讀。

### Passage 3 — for Q7 (跨組織協作 = XD 的具體主張)
> "Strengthen and sustain IMS data collection, and improve coordination between platforms, researchers, and public bodies." 以及 "Ultimately, the IMS framework provides a structured, practical way to improve collective attribution and design more effective countermeasures."

**Page/section**: 建議段
**Why it matters**: 「平台×研究者×公部門協調」「collective attribution」——XD「反詐×反假訊息×平台安全×執法協作」的機構級背書句。

### Passage 4 — for Q8 對照 (具名操作)
> 套用於 Doppelganger、Media Brands/RRN、Undercut、Storm-1516、Overload。

**Page/section**: 案例
**Why it matters**: 提供具名 FIMI 操作清單,Drafter 可用以說明 IMS 在真實操作上的可操作性(對照台灣兩案)。

## Caveats / limitations
- IMS 是 FIMI 導向,strategic 層以 actor+intent 為終點(P frame);本研究主張 I 層才是穩定本體——Drafter 借 IMS 的 operational(infrastructure)層即可,不必照搬其 actor-attribution 終點論。
