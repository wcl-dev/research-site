# c032: 3rd EEAS Report on Foreign Information Manipulation and Interference Threats — Exposing the architecture of FIMI operations (EEAS, 2025-03)

**URL**: https://www.eeas.europa.eu/sites/default/files/documents/2025/EEAS-3nd-ThreatReport-March-2025-05-Digital-HD.pdf
**Source type**: gov (EEAS / EU) | **Quality**: qs=5
**Deep-read on**: 2026-06-16
**Access status**: ok (Layer B-first — gov domain → pdftotext 10.7MB;抽前 14 頁 exec summary)
**Pages/length**: 報告全文(抽 executive summary)

## Directly addresses
- Q2 [共用工業化基礎設施]: 一手繪製 FIMI 的數位基礎設施規模(38,000 channels)與**分層結構**——I 軸「基礎設施作為穩定威脅本體」的機構級權威佐證。
- Q7 [跨域整合框架]: 提出 "FIMI Infrastructure Matrix"(分類與歸因 FIMI 基礎設施的系統方法)——XD 共用分類接口的 EEAS 版。
- Q1/P: 主俄次中的外國歸因(P frame 的代表)。

## Key passages

### Passage 1 — for Q2 (規模關鍵數字,逐字)
> "Applying the Matrix to a sample of 505 FIMI incidents collected and analysed in 2024, involving some 38,000 channels, the report reveals the vast online infrastructure Russia and China use for their FIMI activities." 並 "the targeting of 90 different countries, underscoring the global nature of the FIMI threat" 並 "organisations were targeted in 85% of the incidents, amounting to 322 different organisations."

**Page/section**: Executive summary / Key figures
**Why it matters**: 505 incidents / 38,000 channels / 90 國 / 322 受害組織——I 軸基礎設施工業化規模的一手權威數字。Drafter 可直引作 Q2 規模錨(機構級,高於 preprint)。

### Passage 2 — for Q2/I (分層基礎設施 — I 軸本體論的機構背書)
> "At the core of the FIMI infrastructure lies a central network of channels openly controlled by a threat actor. This backbone of attributed channels ... is composed of multiple layers of networks, each fulfilling distinct roles in the promotion of FIMI activities."

**Page/section**: Infrastructure 分析
**Why it matters**: EEAS 把 FIMI 描述為**多層網絡、各層分工**的基礎設施——這正是本研究 I 軸「基礎設施才是穩定威脅本體」的機構級對應。Drafter 可引以支撐「分析單位應是基礎設施層,不是單則內容」。

### Passage 3 — for Q2/I (資產輪替 — disposable assets)
> "The vast majority of the incidents do not occur on a single platform but tend to be active on multiple fronts ... networks, often short-lived and disposable—active only for a single campaign or swiftly removed by platforms ... Their transient nature makes identification" challenging.

**Page/section**: Infrastructure / detection challenges
**Why it matters**: 「short-lived and disposable」資產、單戰役後即棄——直接對位 brief I 軸的「資產輪替與重新分配」與 Q6「偵測盲區」(transient nature 難以識別)。

### Passage 4 — for Q3 (AI 在 FIMI 的角色 — 誠實標記偵測困難)
> "AI-generated text is also probably used in FIMI operations, but its detection remains challenging, making it difficult to confirm its presence"

**Page/section**: 同上
**Why it matters**: EEAS 對 AI 生成內容**誠實標記不確定**(probably used, detection challenging)——與 c079/c035 一致,可作 Drafter 標記 AI slop 證據邊界的機構級佐證。

## Structural content worth knowing
- **FIMI Infrastructure Matrix**:系統化分類與歸因 FIMI 基礎設施的方法(Nodes=channels/websites,Edges=關係)——XD「共用交換/分類接口」的 EEAS 實例,可與 IMS(c028)、DISARM/STIX(c030/c031)並列。
- Figure 1(2024 incidents 關鍵數字)、Figure 2(頻道分布)——可表格化引用。
- 點名 Doppelganger、Portal Kombat、African Initiative、False Façade 等具名網絡;EU DisinfoLab、Correctiv 亦在受害名單。

## Caveats / limitations
- frame 強烈偏 P(外國性 + 俄/中歸因);其 infrastructure 概念服務 actor attribution 終點。本研究借其**基礎設施分層 + disposable 資產 + Matrix 分類**(I/Q2/XD),但不照搬「FIMI=外國」的歸因框架到「動機可替換」命題。
- 抽 executive summary;細部 Matrix 欄位與案例在後續頁,operator 可按需補抽。
