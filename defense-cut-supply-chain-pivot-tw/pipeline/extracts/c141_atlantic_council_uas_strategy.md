# c141: A Global Strategy to Secure UAS Supply Chains

**URL**: https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/in-depth-research-reports/issue-brief/a-global-strategy-to-secure-uas-supply-chains/
**Source type**: 智庫 issue brief (Atlantic Council) | **Quality**: qs=5
**Deep-read on**: 2026-05-19
**Access status**: ok
**Pages/length**: issue brief

## Directly addresses
- Q5: 美國對中國無人機供應鏈鎖出政策戰略 (Entity List 機制延伸)
- Q2: 全球 DJI / Autel 市占率 baseline

## Key passages

### Passage 1 — for Q5 (中國 UAS 戰略風險表述)
> "the free world has a supply-chain vulnerability problem, as it is dependent on an autocratic adversary for access to UAS for both civilian and military purposes"

> "China's artificially low prices for UAS, achieved through state subsidies, crowd out the development of a homegrown domestic drone industry"

> "the PRC has the ability to restrict US and allied access to UAS, potentially limiting their access in wartime"

**Page/section**: problem statement
**Why it matters**: 對「為什麼非紅供應鏈是國家戰略」給最簡明的論述基底 — 戰時可被切供應。

### Passage 2 — for Q2 (DJI 80% 美國 / 70% 全球)
> "DJI controlling 80 percent of the commercial market within the United States and as much as 70 percent of the global market"

> "Autel, another PRC manufacturer, controlling 7 percent globally"

**Page/section**: 市場份額段
**Why it matters**: DJI 80% (美) / 70% (全球) + Autel 7% = 給 brief「非紅供應鏈訴求 = 對抗中國 ~77% 全球壟斷」一個量化錨。

### Passage 3 — for Q5 (政策建議)
> **Entity List 建議**: "Adding Autel to the Department of Defense's 1260H list, the Commerce Department's entity list, and the Treasury Department's Chinese Military-Industrial Complex Companies List"

> **盟友協同**: "The US State Department should encourage allied governments to do the same, providing reasonable funding measures to accelerate the transition to secure US and allied solutions"

> **產業激勵**: "The United States should provide a variety of incentives, including tax credits and investments, for the research, development, and manufacturing of autonomous vehicles"

**Page/section**: 建議段
**Why it matters**: 「盟友協同 + 1260H + Entity List 延伸」= 美國長臂機制對中國 UAS 廠商的部署藍圖；對台灣處境「若不維持非紅，可能被同類機制波及」有制度根據。

## Structural content worth knowing
- 80% DJI 美國市占 + 7% Autel = 美國非紅化戰略的數據基底
- 1260H list + Entity List + Treasury CMIC list = 三道美方 entity-targeting 法律機制
