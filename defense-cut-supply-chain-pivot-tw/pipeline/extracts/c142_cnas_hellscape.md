# c142: Hellscape for Taiwan: Rethinking Asymmetric Defense

**URL**: https://www.cnas.org/publications/reports/hellscape-for-taiwan
**Source type**: 智庫報告 (CNAS) | **Quality**: qs=5
**Deep-read on**: 2026-05-19
**Access status**: ok
**Pages/length**: 智庫深度報告

## Directly addresses
- Q1: 21萬架無人機 / 1320 艘無人艇規劃的戰略框架 (Hellscape)
- Q2/Q5: 台灣無人機產業的供應鏈瓶頸 + 非紅供應鏈依賴

## Key passages

### Passage 1 — for Q1 (Hellscape 概念)
> "The Hellscape concept of operations leverages dense, multidirectional fires generated by drones and other uncrewed systems to attrite the Chinese invasion fleet and disrupt the PLA's carefully choreographed amphibious landing."

> Required systems include: "Long-range one-way attack (kamikaze) drones, Uncrewed surface vessels (USVs), Uncrewed underwater vehicles (UUVs), Multirotor drones, First-person view (FPV) drones" plus "Mobile surface-to-air missiles (SAMs), Loitering SAMs, Drone interceptors, Gun-based drone defenses, Decoys, Passive defenses."

**Page/section**: 概念段
**Why it matters**: 給「為什麼需要 21 萬架」一個戰略合理化 — 不是亂編數字，是 Hellscape 不對稱作戰概念內含的密集無人載具需求。砍 3350 億等同放棄這個戰略。

### Passage 2 — for Q2/Q5 (台灣產業困境)
> "Taiwan's drone industrial base is inhibited by high manufacturing costs due to the need for non-PRC supply chains, coupled with technological dependency on allies like the United States."

**Page/section**: 產業評估段
**Why it matters**: 雙重困境表述 — (a) 非紅供應鏈成本溢價 (b) 對美國技術依賴 — 對「整機外銷強勁但結構上脆弱」的 nuance 有支撐。

### Passage 3 — for Q2 (本土產業推動)
> "Taiwan has taken major steps to actualize a degree of military-industrial independence by investing in commercial-grade drone production."

> "Taiwan must secure reliable supply chains to independently manufacture the drones required for the Hellscape concept"

**Page/section**: 推動段
**Why it matters**: 「military-industrial independence」目標 — 對被砍 3000 億本土軍工的戰略意義給定義。

### Passage 4 — for Q2/Q6 (政策建議)
> "Taiwanese companies should continue to build relationships with European drone manufacturers and expand the emerging non-PRC drone alliance."

**Page/section**: 建議段
**Why it matters**: 「非紅無人機聯盟」是美方智庫口徑 — 對 brief 「非紅供應鏈」國家品牌承諾的具體 framing。

## Structural content worth knowing
- 必要系統 list 涵蓋 kamikaze drones / USVs / UUVs / multirotor / FPV / SAMs / interceptors / decoys — 是 brief 三大艦隊 / 21 萬架 / 1320 艘的戰略基礎
- 論述兩面：肯定台灣方向，但點出成本 + 美國技術依賴
