# c143: The Drone Supply Chain War: Identifying the Chokepoints to Making a Drone

- 對 brief「非紅供應鏈」訴求的結構性限制

## Key passages

### Passage 1 — for Q2 (五大瓶頸)
> **Structural Materials:** "Carbon fiber reinforced polymer is the skeletal foundation of most unmanned aircraft."

> **Propulsion:** "Neodymium-iron-boron magnets turn electrical current into lift and torque. Each small motor contains roughly 5–15 grams."

> **Batteries:** "Energy storage defines drone endurance limits. Each kilowatt-hour of battery capacity requires between 0.5–1 kilogram."

> **Semiconductors:** Specialized components including "gallium-nitride power amplifiers and infrared detectors."

> **Logistics:** "Composites, magnets, batteries, and semiconductors converge at the final assembly line."

**Page/section**: 五大瓶頸段
**Why it matters**: 給「無人機供應鏈非紅化的難度」具體拆解 — 不是抽象，是 5 個物質類別的供應鏈瓶頸。

### Passage 2 — for Q5 (中國份額具體百分比)
> "About 90 percent of global sintered-magnet output still occurs in China."

> "China processes roughly two-thirds of the world's lithium and more than seventy percent of its graphite anode material."

**Page/section**: 中國份額段
**Why it matters**: 90% 燒結磁鐵 + 66% 鋰加工 + 70% 石墨陽極 — 三個具體數字，對「無人機完全非紅」的不可能性給最有力的量化警示。

### Passage 3 — for Q2/Q5 (烏俄戰場的真相)
> "Every drone involved in the war in Ukraine depends on China. From palm-sized quadcopters...nearly every unmanned system...contains materials and components that originate in Chinese factories."

**Page/section**: 烏克蘭段
**Why it matters**: 直接挑戰「非紅供應鏈」純粹敘事 — 連被視為非紅典範的烏克蘭戰場無人機也未脫中。對 c164/c174「驟雲銳鳶含中國零件」事件提供國際比較基底。

### Passage 4 — for Q2/Q6 (政策建議)
> The article recommends: expanding "coproduction with allies and partners that already lead in critical minerals," establishing "a Five Materials model for defense industry cooperation," creating "modest government or consortium-managed reserves," and developing stockpiles of "rare-earth magnets, carbon-fiber prepregs, and lithium-ion precursors."

**Page/section**: 建議段
**Why it matters**: 「Five Materials model」+ 戰略儲備建議 — 給台灣的政策建議錨點 (雖文中未具名台灣)。
