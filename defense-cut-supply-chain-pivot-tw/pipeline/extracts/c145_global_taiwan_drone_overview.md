# c145: Taiwan's Emerging Indigenous Drone Industry — An Overview

**URL**: https://globaltaiwan.org/2026/02/tw-drone-production/
**Source type**: 智庫評論 (Global Taiwan Institute) | **Quality**: qs=5

## Directly addresses
- Q2: B 軸最完整 overview — 產能目標 + 廠商 + 認證 + 訂單規模
- Q5: 中國 90% drone 市占率 + 80% 稀土的結構性壓力
- counter-framing「外銷對沖」具體成本溢價 (25% 比中國貴)

## Key passages

### Passage 1 — for Q2 (產能目標 + 實績)
> "Taiwan is aiming to become a drone manufacturing powerhouse" with a goal to produce "180,000 drone units a year by 2028."

> Between June 2024 and June 2025, "Taiwanese producers manufactured approximately 10,000 UAV units. By September, exports alone grew to about 50,000."

> Taiwan "exported approximately 26,000 drones between January and July 2025—a 749 percent increase from 2024."

**Page/section**: 產能段
**Why it matters**: 給「21萬架軍購預算」一個 production capacity context — 2028 目標 18 萬架 / 年。被砍 21 萬架 = 接近一年產能全部訂單蒸發。749% 同期成長對 21/41.7 倍敘事補強。

### Passage 2 — for Q2 (廠商 list 與認證)
> Major contenders for contracts include "Thunder Tiger, Coretronic, and Aerospace Industrial Development Corporation (AIDC)."

> "Thunder Tiger has been able to achieve the US Department of Defense's Defense Innovation Unit (DIU) Blue UAS certification."

> Thunder Tiger achieved "a prominent co-production agreement with Virginia-based UAV firm RapidFlight."

> AIDC heads "the Taiwan Excellence Drone Industry Business Opportunities Alliance (TEDIBOA)" which "oversees and coordinates with a group of over 200 Taiwanese companies."

**Page/section**: 廠商段
**Why it matters**: 三大主戰力具名 + Blue UAS 認證細節 + 200 家 TEDIBOA 聯盟 — 給 brief「200 家無人機廠商」的具體 sourcing。

### Passage 3 — for Q5 (中國份額)
> "Taiwan can mass-produce advanced chips and many dual-use components necessary for drone production, there are still parts that it cannot source on its own."

> China "dominates the drone market, with about 90 percent of the global market share" and controls over "80 percent" of rare earth metals globally.

> "PRC espionage risks pose a major hurdle in securing these agreements" for coproduction.

**Page/section**: 供應鏈段
**Why it matters**: 三個量化錨點 — 90% 全球無人機市場 + 80% 稀土 + PRC espionage 風險 — 對「非紅供應鏈無法純粹」的結構性壓力。

### Passage 4 — for counter-framing「外銷對沖」(關鍵 nuance)
> Taiwan "sells its drones at prices only 25 percent more than Chinese-made ones" despite maintaining China-free supply chains.

> "Maintaining a non-red supply chain is not cheap."

**Page/section**: 成本段
**Why it matters**: 25% 成本溢價 = 非紅供應鏈的具體 price tag。對「外銷對沖」的 nuance: 不是無代價，但溢價有限 (25%) 因此可賣。對「砍訂單後外銷能否補」評估的關鍵變數。

### Passage 5 — for Q1 (官方訂單規模)
> July 2025: "NTD 50 billion (USD 1.68 billion) order for nearly 50,000 military drones" with "11,270 units delivered in 2026 and 37,480 units delivered in 2027."

> October 2025: Orders for "over 50,000 additional domestically-produced dual-use drones" for civil government agencies.

**Page/section**: 訂單段
**Why it matters**: 已下訂的 500 億 + 5 萬架 軍方訂單 + 5 萬架民用訂單 — 對「被砍 21 萬架」的相對基線。被砍預算 = 約 4 倍既有訂單規模消失。

## Structural content worth knowing
- 完整時間線 (June 2024 - July 2025) + 廠商 + 訂單規模 + 成本溢價 + 中國市占 — 是 B 軸最 self-contained 的 overview，Drafter 可重度倚賴
- 「Thunder Tiger / Coretronic / AIDC」= 三大主戰力具名出處
- TEDIBOA 200 家聯盟 = 廠商生態系規模

## Caveats / limitations
- 8 月 / 9 月差異敘述有些不一致 (50,000 exports 跟 26,000 + 749%) — Drafter 引用時擇一即可
- 主要視角是美國觀察者，對台灣本土工具機 (A 軸) 西進壓力未談
