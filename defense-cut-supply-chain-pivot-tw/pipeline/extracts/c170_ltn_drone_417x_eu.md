## Key passages

### Passage 1 — for Q2 (核心數字)
> "Taiwan's drone exports to Europe grew 41.7-fold from 2024 to last year"

**Page/section**: lead 段
**Why it matters**: 「41.7 倍」的具體出處 (相比 c133 整機 21 倍是 2024→2025 全球，c170 41.7 倍是專指對歐洲的成長) — 兩個數字並非互斥。

### Passage 2 — for Q2 / Q6 (烏俄戰爭因果)
> "with demand from Ukraine's fight against Russian aggression the most likely driver of growth"

> "These countries likely transferred the drones to Ukraine to aid it in its fight against the Russian invasion that started in 2022"

**Page/section**: 中段
**Why it matters**: 給「波蘭 + 捷克 = 轉口烏克蘭」這個 brief E 軸路徑一個明確 sourceable 陳述。

### Passage 3 — for Q2 (轉口路徑)
> "it found that many of Taiwan's uncrewed aerial vehicle (UAV) sales were from Poland and the Czech Republic"

> "Many drones exported from Taiwan to Central and Eastern Europe are procured by private Ukrainian actors and then routed to Ukraine"

**Page/section**: 中段
**Why it matters**: 「私人烏克蘭實體採購 → 轉送烏克蘭」是供應鏈研究上的關鍵細節 — 不是 G2G 軍售而是 private actor routing。

## Structural content worth knowing
- 研究來源：DSET (民主社會與新興科技研究所) — 不是政府數據，是智庫研究
- 41.7 倍 vs 21 倍 並非衝突：21 倍是整機產值全球外銷 (CNA c133)、41.7 倍是「對歐洲」單一市場 (本篇)

## Caveats / limitations
- 文章未提供 41.7 倍對應的「絕對金額」基數 — 若 2024 基數很小，41.7 倍實際金額有限
- 「private Ukrainian actors」路徑沒具名公司 — 不能個案展示
