## Key passages

### Passage 1 — for Q2 (abstract summary)
> "In an era where language models are increasingly integrated into decision-making and communication, understanding the biases within Large Language Models (LLMs) becomes imperative, especially when these models are applied in the economic and political domain."

> Introduces PoliTune, "employing Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) techniques, which allow for the alignment of LLMs with targeted ideologies by modifying a small subset of parameters."

**Page/section**: Abstract
**Why it matters**: Establishes that political ideology can be efficiently injected into LLMs through fine-tuning — supports the claim that Chinese LLM censorship is a deliberate fine-tuning choice, not an unavoidable consequence of training data.

### Passage 2 — for Q2 (fine-tuning enables deliberate political alignment)
> The research demonstrates that "embedding specific biases into LLMs" through fine-tuning is feasible using parameter-efficient methods, showing that deliberate data selection and alignment techniques systematically shape model outputs toward particular ideological positions.

**Page/section**: Key findings
**Why it matters**: Proves that political alignment is an active design choice, not a passive emergent property — essential for the advocacy argument that Chinese LLM censorship is intentional state-directed policy.

### Passage 3 — for Q2 (intentionality and ethics)
> The authors emphasize "the ethical application of AI, highlighting the importance of deploying AI in a manner that aligns with societal values," suggesting fine-tuning choices represent intentional design decisions with significant consequences.

**Page/section**: Implications
**Why it matters**: Frames fine-tuning as a consequential design choice with ethical dimensions — provides the normative foundation for arguing that Chinese LLM developers are accountable for their identity-triggered censorship policies.

## Structural content worth knowing
- Uses Llama3-8B and Mistral-7B as experimental models; Llama3-70B for annotation.
- DPO (Direct Preference Optimization) fine-tuning method used.
- PEFT (Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning) makes ideological alignment achievable with minimal compute — lowers the barrier for state actors to implement political alignment.

## Caveats / limitations
- Full text not accessible from AAAI OJS.
- Paper uses Western political dimensions (left-right ideology) not geopolitical/national dimensions — Drafter must make the inferential extension explicit.
- Shows how easily bias can be embedded; doesn't prove that Chinese developers used these specific techniques.
