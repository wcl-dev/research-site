## Key passages

### Passage 1 — for Q3 (back-end tool access, verbatim)
> "One of the latest efforts against disinformation is a unique collaboration between Facebook and the Taiwan FactCheck Center. The nonprofit center uses a back-end tool provided by Facebook to track viral, misleading posts and works to fact-check them. Once it does, Facebook alerts anyone who shared the post that it was wrong and includes a link to the fact-check article below the false post."

**Why it matters**: This is the earliest-dated, most concrete confirmation in this project's evidence base that TFC's Facebook collaboration included **direct platform tool access** (not just a submit-and-wait review relationship) — TFC used Facebook-provided tooling to independently discover viral misleading content, not only to review content flagged to it. This "tool access" is precisely the asset category that CrowdTangle's 2024 closure and Meta Content Library's non-profit-only, commercial-media-excluding gating (both already documented in seeds) later constrained — c005 supplies the pre-2020-election baseline of what a fact-checking partner's tool access looked like before that contraction.

## Structural content worth knowing
