## Caveats / limitations
- **Same-actor discipline reminder for the Drafter**: this letter is authored by IFCN/the signatory fact-checkers, **not by Meta**. It is legitimate B/C-concept evidence of the fact-checking community's reaction, but it is not itself a Meta courtship or exit statement, and must not be blended into a "Meta said X" quote.
- Per brief Existing knowledge §2.4, the letter's plea for continuation is **not** usable as evidence that the 3PFC relationship was a good deal for fact-checkers ("鋼人已陣亡論證" — budget-dependency-driven pleas for continuation prove negotiating weakness, not a demonstrated preference for the arrangement's fairness).
