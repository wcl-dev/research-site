# c017: TFC「接受開放社會基金會（OSF）捐款說明」

## Directly addresses
- Q6 [D, background]: confirms exactly when OSF funding stopped, feeding "how thin is the alternative funding after Meta withdraws" question.
- Q3 [B]: gives a **second, independent-of-Nikkei** TFC statement that Meta = roughly half of annual revenue — this time in TFC's own explanatory prose, not a ledger table (complements c016's table with running prose confirmation).

## Key passages

### Passage 1 — for Q6 (total OSF funding, verbatim)
> "從2021年到2024年，TFC從喬治・索羅斯名下慈善機構所收到的捐款共為14,487,800元。"

**Why it matters**: Confirms the aggregate 2021-2024 OSF total (NT$14,487,800) in TFC's own words, matching the gate record's year-by-year breakdown (2021: 1,396,801; 2022: 151,950+8,214,259; 2023: 2,362,395; 2024: 2,362,395 pending audit).

### Passage 2 — for Q6 (funding cutoff date, verbatim — the key Q6 data point)
> "雖然2024年下半年之後，OSF已停止對TFC捐款，但過去幾年的經費贊助，對TFC組織營運及台灣民主與公平選舉的助益極大。捐款事實除已於歷年陸續揭露於官網，藉此再申謝忱。"

**Why it matters**: This is the precise, TFC-confirmed answer to "when did OSF funding stop" — **second half of 2024**, roughly six months before Meta's Jan 2025 US termination announcement and about 18 months before the Dec 2025 Taiwan-specific reporting on Meta's own funding risk. Useful for the brief's Q6 "上下游雙黑箱" one-line context: TFC's OSF funding line was already closing before the Meta situation became acute — i.e. the funding-diversification pressure on TFC was compounding from two directions on different timelines, not a single simultaneous shock.

### Passage 3 — for Q3 (Meta = half of revenue, in TFC's own explanatory prose — independent phrasing from the c016 table)
> "自2018年開始營運以來，台灣事實查核中心的營運經費均來自企業與個人捐款、內容授權，以及從2019年開始與Meta合作進行的「第三方查證計畫」，其中與Meta的合作收入佔本中心全年收入的半數。"

**Why it matters**: A second, differently-worded TFC confirmation that Meta = roughly half of annual revenue — this is explanatory prose in an OSF-focused explainer page, not the donation-report table (c016), giving the Drafter two independently-phrased primary statements to draw on rather than one, both converging on "Meta ≈ half."

### Passage 4 — for Q6 (USAID rumor rebuttal, verbatim, mixed-language quote)
> "經本中心直接詢問當初負責OSF東亞地區業務、負責與本中心接洽的人士，該筆捐款是否來自USAID？獲其回覆：「贊助來自『開放社會基金會』，也就是來自喬治・索羅斯先生」，並直斥網傳說法是「假訊息」（It is fake news.）。"

**Why it matters**: TFC directly investigated and rebutted a USAID-funding rumor about itself — confirms funding-source transparency practice and demonstrates TFC actively countering misinformation about its own funding (a nice, if minor, self-referential agency data point for Q5). The English phrase "It is fake news." is embedded verbatim mid-sentence in the zh-TW original — confirmed exactly as quoted, not a translation artifact I introduced.

## Structural content worth knowing
